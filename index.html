Optimize yield, capacity and time-to-market across company lines.

Data barriers leave yield, capacity and time-to-market on the table. Multyx optimizes across companies without exposing any party's inputs to the others: each gets its own result, with its commercial interests and IP protected.

Value on the table

The value is measurable. The data behind it sits across company lines.

Typically, foundries share WAT data, not inline and tool data. Customers share bin results, not test programs. So yield learning, excursion containment and root cause stop at the company line.

Illustrative industry figures

Yield~$150M a year

One point of yield at a 10nm, 60k-wafer-a-month logic fab.1

Time-to-market~$29M

Lost when a $1B product with a 2-year window ships one week late.2

Capacity~600 wafers a month

The same yield point counted as output: more good wafers with no new tool.3

Process savings~$0.75M

At risk for each 25-wafer lot an excursion reaches before containment.4

Cost of non-quality, priced in yield, weeks and wafers.

Industry-level figures, not Multyx results. Sources differ in year and node; they show orders of magnitude, not one fab's P&L. 1. Entegris (YICAI, Nov 2019): about $150M profit a year per one point of yield at a 10nm logic fab running 60k wafer starts a month. 2. Triangle revenue model (Prasad 1997, via Arteris 2011): one week late on a 2-year window ≈ 2.86% of lifetime revenue. 3. 60,000 wafer starts a month × one point of yield = 600 good-wafer equivalents a month. 4. ~$30k is a press estimate of a leading foundry's 2nm customer wafer price (TechNode, Oct 2025), not fab cost. 25 × $30k = $0.75M.
